# TechMart Store Policies

## Return & Refund Policy

### Standard Return Window
- Customers may return eligible items within **30 days** of the delivery date.
- Items must be in their **original, unused condition** and in **original packaging** with all accessories, manuals, and documentation included.
- A valid proof of purchase (order number or receipt) is required for all returns.

### Non-Returnable Items
- Opened software licenses and digital download codes are **non-refundable**.
- Items marked as **"Final Sale"** at time of purchase cannot be returned.
- Consumable products (batteries, ink cartridges) that have been opened cannot be returned.
- Custom-configured or built-to-order systems cannot be returned unless defective.

### Defective or Damaged Items
- If an item arrives damaged or defective, contact us within **7 days** of delivery.
- We will arrange a free return and provide a full refund or replacement at no extra cost.
- Photos of damage may be required for processing.

### Return Process
1. Log into your TechMart account and go to "My Orders".
2. Select the order and click "Initiate Return".
3. Choose the item(s) and reason for return.
4. Print the prepaid return shipping label (provided via email).
5. Drop off the package at any authorised courier location.

### Refund Processing
- Refunds are processed within **5–7 business days** after we receive and inspect the returned item.
- Refunds are issued to the **original payment method**.
- Credit card refunds may take an additional 3–5 business days to appear depending on your bank.
- Gift card payments are refunded as store credit.

## Warranty Policy

### Manufacturer Warranty
- All TechMart products are covered by the **manufacturer's warranty** as specified on each product page.
- Warranty periods vary by product (see product listing for details): typically 1–3 years.
- Warranty covers **manufacturing defects** and hardware failures under normal use.
- Warranty does **not** cover physical damage, water damage, or damage from misuse.

### Extended Warranty (TechMart Care)
- Extended warranty plans are available at checkout for most electronics.
- Plans extend coverage by 1 or 2 additional years beyond the manufacturer warranty.
- TechMart Care also includes **accidental damage protection** (screen cracks, liquid spills).
- Cost: 10–15% of product price depending on product category.

### Warranty Claims
- Warranty claims must be initiated through TechMart's support portal or the manufacturer's website.
- For Apple products: visit apple.com/support or an Apple Authorised Service Provider.
- For other brands: contact TechMart Support and we will coordinate with the manufacturer.
- Proof of purchase is always required for warranty claims.
