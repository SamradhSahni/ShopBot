# TechMart Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders & Purchasing

**Q: How do I place an order?**
A: Browse products, add items to your cart, proceed to checkout, enter your shipping address and payment details, then confirm your order. You'll receive a confirmation email immediately.

**Q: Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?**
A: Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After that, the order enters processing and cannot be changed. Contact support immediately if you need to cancel.

**Q: Do I need an account to make a purchase?**
A: No, guest checkout is available. However, creating an account allows you to track orders, save addresses, and access order history.

**Q: Is there a minimum order value?**
A: No minimum order value. However, orders under $50 incur a $4.99 standard shipping fee.

**Q: Can I use multiple discount codes on one order?**
A: Only one discount code can be applied per order. Gift cards can be combined with a discount code.

---

## Shipping & Delivery

**Q: How do I track my order?**
A: Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also log into your account and go to "My Orders" to see real-time tracking.

**Q: What if my package is lost or stolen?**
A: Contact our support team within 7 days. We will investigate with the carrier and either reship the item or issue a refund.

**Q: Do you offer free shipping?**
A: Yes! Standard shipping is free on all orders over $50 within the United States.

**Q: Can I ship to a P.O. Box?**
A: Standard shipping can deliver to P.O. Boxes. Express and Overnight shipping require a physical address.

**Q: What happens if I'm not home for delivery?**
A: The carrier will make up to 3 delivery attempts. After that, the package is held at a local facility for 5 business days before being returned.

---

## Returns & Refunds

**Q: How do I start a return?**
A: Log into your account, go to "My Orders", select the item, and click "Initiate Return". You'll receive a prepaid return label via email.

**Q: Can I return an opened product?**
A: Yes, as long as it's within 30 days and the product is in resalable condition with all original accessories. Opened software and digital codes are not returnable.

**Q: I received a damaged item. What should I do?**
A: Contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the damage. We'll arrange a free return and full refund or replacement.

**Q: How long does it take to receive my refund?**
A: Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after we receive the returned item. Credit card refunds appear within an additional 3–5 business days depending on your bank.

**Q: Can I exchange a product instead of returning it?**
A: Currently, we don't offer direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order for the desired product.

## Products & Warranty

**Q: How do I know if a product is in stock?**
A: Product availability is shown on each product page. "In Stock" means ready to ship, "Low Stock" means limited units available, and "Out of Stock" means currently unavailable.

**Q: Where can I find the warranty information for a product?**
A: Warranty details are listed on each product page under the "Specifications" section and also included in the product box.

**Q: How do I make a warranty claim?**
A: Contact TechMart Support with your order number and a description of the issue. We'll guide you through the manufacturer's warranty process.

**Q: Does TechMart offer its own extended warranty?**
A: Yes, TechMart Care extended warranty plans are available at checkout. They extend coverage by 1–2 additional years and may include accidental damage protection.

**Q: Are your products genuine/authentic?**
A: 100%. TechMart is an authorised retailer for all brands we carry. All products come with full manufacturer warranty and documentation.

## Payment & Security

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, TechMart Gift Cards, and Affirm (Buy Now Pay Later).

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**
A: Yes. We use TLS 1.3 encryption and are PCI-DSS Level 1 certified. We never store your full card number.

**Q: Do you offer financing or instalment payments?**
A: Yes, through Affirm. Eligible customers can get 0% APR for 6 months on orders over $200. Available at checkout.

**Q: What is the Price Match Guarantee?**
A: If you find an identical product in stock at an authorised retailer at a lower price within 14 days of purchase, we'll match it. Submit a request through your account or contact support.

## Technical Support

**Q: Do you offer technical support for purchased products?**
A: TechMart offers basic setup support for 30 days after purchase. For extended support, TechMart Care plans include priority technical assistance.

**Q: Where can I find product manuals?**
A: Digital manuals are available on each product page under "Downloads". Physical manuals are included in the product box.

**Q: Can I get help setting up my new laptop/TV/device?**
A: Yes. Chat or call our support team within 30 days of purchase for free setup assistance.
